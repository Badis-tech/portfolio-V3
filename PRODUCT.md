# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **International freelance clients**, English-first: deciding whether to hire Badis for a SaaS, automation or data project.
- **Tunisian and Moroccan recruiters**, French-first: HR staff and tech leads at local companies and IT service firms (ESN) screening a full-stack candidate, usually alongside his CV.

Both readers are evaluating fast: who he is, what he has shipped, whether they can see it working, and how to contact him.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Badis Mahjoubi, full-stack & AI developer based in Tunis. Success means a visitor opens a live demo or downloads the CV, then makes contact (email or LinkedIn).

## Positioning
Every project card links to something you can try: the live AusbildungJobs SaaS, or an interactive mockup deployed on Vercel. The site shows working software, not just descriptions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 15 (App Router), Tailwind CSS v4, framer-motion; deployed on Vercel.
- EN / FR / AR with RTL support; English is the default locale. Dark and light themes.
- CV PDFs (EN and FR) in `public/cv/`, generated by `scripts/generate_cv.py`.
- Project data, demo links and screenshots live in `lib/data.ts` (`projectDefs`) and `public/projects/`.
- Mockups are front-end demos with fictional data and must stay labelled as such.

## Evidence on Hand
- Live product: AusbildungJobs (jobs-pipe-line.vercel.app).
- Six interactive mockups: erp-plasturgie, erp-grossiste, erp-elevage, visa-hunter, pybalancer, mini-prometheus (`<name>.vercel.app`).
- Education: ESPRIT engineering degree (Fintech, 2020–2025), ISET Radès (2016–2020).
- No testimonials, client logos or quantified client results exist; never invent them.
- Open: the Experience entries (Tontec, CODIX) currently conflict with the CV and await confirmation from Badis.

## Product Principles
1. Show, don't claim: every project should be openable.
2. Honest about scope: mockups are labelled, metrics are real or absent.
3. Fast to scan: a recruiter should get role, stack, proof and contact within one screen.
4. Bilingual by default: no content lives in only one language.
